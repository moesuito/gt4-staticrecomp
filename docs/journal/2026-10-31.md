## TASK-041: Low-end hardware and integrated graphics performance study (Issues #112, #97, #126)

- Forensics and performance study authored in docs/LOW-END-HARDWARE-PERF-STUDY.md.
- Analyzed upstream issue reports:
  - Issue #112: Intel Core i3-1005G1 (Intel UHD Graphics, Ice Lake, 32 EUs, 15W TDP) - 60 FPS in general menus, but severe drops to ~45 FPS when cars are previewed on showroom turntables and at 6-car race starts.
  - Issue #97: Intel Core i5-3337U (Intel HD Graphics 4000, Ivy Bridge, 16 EUs) - stuttering and freezes in showroom/races; low-end hardware profile requested.
  - Issue #126: Performance regression report on lower-tier hardware.
  - Owner local report (2026-10-26): Intel Core i5-7200U (Intel HD Graphics 620, Kaby Lake, 24 EUs, 15W TDP) - video pacing contention during opening FMV and title video; solid 60 FPS in 3D races with Performance Mode enabled.
- Key findings and bottleneck mechanics:
  - Memory bandwidth and shared TDP limits: on 15W ULV packages, CPU and iGPU compete for shared DDR3/DDR4 bandwidth (12-25 GB/s vs 200+ GB/s on discrete GPUs). Heavy GPU fillrate load causes CPU/GPU thermal throttle.
  - Showroom turntable reflection passes: car body multi-pass specular reflections, real-time environment cubemap updates, high-polygon meshes, and consecutive render target switches drive 100% iGPU EU saturation.
  - Race starts: peak primitive and drawcall density with 6 vehicles, dynamic shadow projections, light glow sprites, and motion blur.
  - Audio stuttering mechanism: audio latency target in 
ative/host_io.cpp is 60 ms; when frametimes spike to 35-50 ms, audio ring buffers drain completely before next presentation, producing audible crackling/dropouts.
- Mitigation evaluation and recommendations:
  - TASK-105 native 1x resolution default reduces fragment shading work by ~89%, maintaining baseline 60 FPS on lightweight scenes.
  - Performance Mode (HWDownloadMode = 2 no-readbacks) eliminates readback sync stalls.
  - Direct3D 11 API on Intel graphics bypasses Vulkan CPU submission overhead and driver quirks.
  - Roadmap items: dynamic audio buffer elasticity under heavy load, showroom reflection pass LOD (30 Hz refresh rate for cubemaps), and low-end presets.
