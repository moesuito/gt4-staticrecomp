## TASK-140: Triage of Issue #135 (Bonnet Cam wind sound & 480p color gamut)

Owner requested addressing upstream Issue #135: "Option to disable Wind sound in Bonnet Cam - Full Range of color on 480p".

### Technical Findings & Triage Analysis

1. **Full Range of Color on 480p:**
   - Gran Turismo 4's progressive 480p mode (DTV 480p, 525p) switches the GS display circuit to non-interlaced scan. In progressive mode, the game disables 480i dithering and adjusts internal post-processing for analog YPbPr component video (limited 16-235 TV levels).
   - On PC monitors displaying raw RGB output without TV contrast curve expansion, this produces a flatter, washed-out image with lifted black levels and lower perceived color saturation compared to 480i.
   - `gt4-gs.exe` already provides the PCSX2 GS post-processing pipeline: **ShadeBoost**.
   - In GT4Recomp, `shade_boost` is already defined in `docs/launcher/gs-settings.toml` and supported under **Settings > Graphics > Advanced**:
     - `Shade boost`: toggle on.
     - `Shade boost contrast`: increase (55-60) to deepen blacks and widen dynamic range.
     - `Shade boost saturation`: increase (55-60) to restore rich color gamut.
     - `Shade boost brightness` / `Shade boost gamma`: optional fine adjustment.
   - Enabling ShadeBoost resolves the 480p washed-out appearance on digital displays without altering game code or guest shaders.

2. **Option to disable Wind sound in Bonnet Cam:**
   - In GT4, camera views (bumper, bonnet, roof, chase) alter the SPU2 guest sound engine mix. The bonnet (hood) perspective incorporates an intentional rushing wind sound effect at speed designed by Polyphony Digital.
   - Under `AGENTS.md` (Mission and direction), GT4Recomp's core objective is faithful native recompilation:
     - "Out of scope here: a decompilation-based source port, changes to the game's physics timing and remaster features will be a separate project, possibly a fork. This repository's goal is the original game running natively, optimized and as faithful as possible."
     - "Game logic never changes: physics, timing, AI, saves, and anything the EE or IOP reads back that affects gameplay stay exact"
   - Selectively stripping individual sound design layers from the guest audio engine is an audio mod / remaster feature out of scope for the core native recompiler. Bumper camera remains available as an original perspective with direct mechanical audio and without the bonnet wind layer. Custom sound mix alterations are noted for future community modding forks.
