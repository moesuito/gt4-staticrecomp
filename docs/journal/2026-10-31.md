## TASK-027: closing 1x on the HD 620 laptop

All numbers: Time Trial on the Nordschleife by day, 1x, performance mode, i5-7200U with HD 620, candidate 362 unless noted.

- **What presenting costs (RODAR6, window against no window, with the CPU profile, same 1500 VBlanks, shaders ready).**
  The present calls themselves are small: about 0.5 ms on the GS thread (driver 0.35, signal 0.15), GPU 8.08 against 7.72 ms.
  But every thread takes more CPU time per VBlank with the window: GS 16.0 against 14.1, VU1 12.5 against 11.1, EE 10.8
  against 9.0, and the same functions cost more (CT32 swizzle +31%, `BlockOfPixel` +29%). Hypotheses, not yet measured:
  the pacing wait (threads go to sleep and wake on cold caches), the desktop compositor on the second thread of a core,
  memory shared with the GPU. RODAR7 runs the cells: window, window without the wait, no window, vsync.
  The profile itself costs more with a window (68 against 41 us suspended per visit at 1 kHz).
- **The rhythm of spikes.** Pairs of frames slower every 460-500 ms, in both runs, at a fixed time and not at a fixed frame.
  It also shows after the sampler stops. Unknown.
- **Falling FPS during the race, measured:** with a window, about 59.5 VBlanks/s for the first 25 s of the race, then 58, 57, 56,
  54.5, 51.6. Hypothesis: the end of the 28 s turbo window of a 15 W processor (the clock falls from 114% to about 103%,
  3079 to 2780 MHz, at about that moment) together with a heavier part of the track: without a window the frame also grows from 10
  to 15 ms over the same VBlanks, and VU1 from 6 to 8.8 ms. This may be the owner's drop against the sun; not separated yet.
- **GS cuts:** IN-485 palettes (GS 13.70 to 11.91 ms), IN-486 uploads (-0.45), IN-487 block lookup. Frame without a window now 11-12.5 ms.
  IN-488: fixing threads to cores gains nothing. Gates of the build with IN-485 (128 buckets), IN-486 and IN-487: 3x night 10/10, Arcade 14/14 identical.
- **The owner's idea of preparing shaders is right:** IN-489. A new executable means 13.7 ms of GPU instead of 8 for its first
  sessions. The list and the cache are in; shipping a first list and a launcher screen are not done.
- **Frame skip:** the report of TASK-082 (`origin/task/082-render-skip`) puts the flashing old frames on the game's page flip: a skipped
  frame still flips, so the display returns to the page that kept the old frame (hypothesis there, no runtime trace). Under CoreGS 2.0 the fix has to act before the flip's privileged write.
