# 2026-10-31

## TASK-130: Split-screen resolution and aspect ratio triage (issue #92)

- Triaged community Issue #92 regarding split-screen resolution quality and inability to select 4:3
  (horizontal split-screen mode).
- Problem & Findings ([SPLIT-SCREEN-STUDY](docs/SPLIT-SCREEN-STUDY.md)):
  - In Gran Turismo 4, 2-player battle has two viewport modes:
    * 4:3 (Normal mode): Horizontal split (top and bottom viewports), each 640x224 pixels.
    * 16:9 (Wide mode): Vertical split (left and right viewports), each 320x448 pixels.
  - The 4:3 lockout: `TASK-079` (`GT4_FORCE_WIDESCREEN=1`) forces the `MOption` widescreen flag in guest RAM at
    every VBlank. This immediately overwrites in-game screen option toggles back to 16:9 at VBlank.
  - Perceived quality: On PS2 hardware, each player's viewport resolution was halved to maintain 60 FPS within
    the 4MB GS eDRAM bandwidth. In GSdx, upscaled rendering (e.g. 3x) scales from this half-resolution buffer,
    resulting in lower texture and polygon density than single-player.
  - Pillarboxing: `native/display_aspect.cpp` returns early on split-screen canvases, keeping the scene policy
    in 16:9 pillarbox mode rather than stretching across ultrawide displays.
- Architecture Roadmap:
  - Documented requirements for two-player controller mapping (`pad1`/`pad2`) under the controls backlog (TASK-018 / TASK-123).
  - Documented future CoreGS 2.0 / 3.0 native viewport resolution scaling to decouple split-screen from PS2 eDRAM limits.
