# 2026-10-31

## TASK-045: high-FPS presentation & GSdx decoupling roadmap

- Re-evaluated and updated high-framerate presentation requirements (TASK-045, redone from TASK-031 for the Decision 0070 separate-process GSdx architecture).
- Non-negotiable constraint: game physics simulation, tire friction, suspension, and vehicle dynamics remain 100% exact and fixed at 60 Hz (`RaceBase__updateRace`, constant `dt = 1/60`).
- Established 4-tier presentation architecture:
  - Mode 1 (VRR / Mailbox Swap): presents finished 60 Hz frames immediately on FreeSync/G-Sync monitors without waiting for 60 Hz VSync boundaries, reducing display latency by up to ~12 ms.
  - Mode 2 (Decoupled GS-Stream Primitive Interpolation): holds frame $N+1$, matches geometry streams, interpolates screen-space primitives at $t = 0.5$ (120 Hz) or $t = 0.4, 0.8$ (144 Hz), and transmits `AUX_FRAME` across the shared-memory IPC ring to `gt4-gs.exe` (+1 frame buffer latency, ~16.7 ms, real rasterized geometry).
  - Mode 3 (Late-Stage Camera Reprojection): compute pass space-warping previous frame from depth and camera matrices.
  - Mode 4 (Host Driver / Interoperability): compatibility with host-level presentation hooks.
- Mapped milestone plan: M1 VRR Controls in Launcher, M2 Offline Primitive Oracle (`tools/gs_interp`), M3 Protocol Extension (`STREAM_OP_AUX_FRAME`), M4 Auxiliary Dispatch in Engine, M5 Pacing Scheduler in `native/pacing.cpp`.
- Authored comprehensive roadmap: `docs/TASK-045-HIGH-FPS-ROADMAP.md`.
- Updated `TASKS.md` and `docs/STATUS.md`.
