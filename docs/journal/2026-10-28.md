## Owner test 13b and the deep performance plan

- Owner test 13b (candidate 287): sharpness solved ("nitidez 100% resolvida"); the game's effects look right. The
  night B-Spec City races are still heavy: 35 FPS on the owner's overlay with several cars facing the camera, GPU at
  51% and 34 W.
- Night timings from the owner's session log: 22.7-23.6 ms per VBlank for the whole emulator. The GS thread runs
  16-18 ms, of which only about 9 ms is CPU time; 7.7 ms is spent in readbacks.
- Most readbacks are ours, not the game's: about 2.9 per VBlank are CLUT loads over a drawn target, and each copies
  the target's whole drawn area to the CPU. The EE itself asks for a download only about 0.2-0.7 times per VBlank.
- Other measurements: 236 texture decodes per VBlank against 97 cache hits; 5.4 targets created and 5.4 replaced per
  VBlank; live image memory grew from 177 MB to 1 GB over the session.
- Plan (CORE2-ROADMAP phase B, revised):
  - B0: a headless night scene and a `.gscap`, plus a per-frame timeline;
  - B1: CLUT and indexed loads from targets on the GPU, with no CPU wait, and the EE downloads reduced to their
    rectangle;
  - B2: the texture cache;
  - B3: targets and memory;
  - B4: the front end;
  - B5: a second GS worker, if needed;
  - B6: Spec II (George V Paris) as the stress scene after B1.
- VU1 on the GPU stays a CoreGS 3.0 item, since the measured cost sits in the GS thread. The owner cannot save a
  replay now.
- Owner direction after the plan: 16.7 ms is the ceiling, not the goal. Cut the load as far as possible, the waits
  first, because an unlocked frame rate (through frame interpolation) and the mobile port come later. The beta gate is
  the owner's laptop, an i5-7200U with Intel HD 620, at 2x (720p) at full speed. Budgets are recorded in
  CORE2-ROADMAP phase B; on the desktop at 3x in the night scene: GS thread at or under 3 ms of CPU, no full-queue
  waits, the whole emulator at or under 10 ms.

## Phase B starts: the night race scene, palettes on the GPU (IN-407..IN-410, candidate 288)

- **The night test scene, found by a subagent with headless runs.** The owner's suggestion was used: a night B-Spec
  City race.
  - GT Mode, Beginner Events, FF Challenge race 3 (Hong Kong Reverse), VW Lupo 1.4, B-Spec, 5 AI cars.
  - Card: a copy of `private/hwr-play-274/card.ps2`, which the game never writes.
  - First race camera at VBlank 6500, green flag at about 7300.
  - The runs are deterministic: frames are byte-identical from 7200 on.
  - Presses (`--press frame:button:frames`, `--frames 11500`): `600:down:10 700:cross:30 1100:cross:10
    1400:cross:10 1950:down:8 2050:down:8 2150:cross:10 2350:cross:10 2600:triangle:10 2900:triangle:10
    3200:down:8 3350:right:8 3500:cross:10 3620:down:8 3700:cross:10 3900:cross:10 4900:up:8 5050:right:8
    5200:right:8 5500:down:8 5650:cross:10 5850:cross:10 6300:right:8 6450:cross:10`.
  - Run it with `GT4_RENDERER=hw GT4_HW_CORE=2 GT4_FORCE_480P=1 GT4_FORCE_WIDESCREEN=1 GT4_HW_SCALE=3`.
- **What the night trace showed:**
  - GT4 computes palettes on the GPU (textured fans and sprites that sample themselves, a 64 x 96 palette atlas read
    by about 85 CLUT loads per VBlank).
  - It reads its 128 x 128 night render as a T8 texture.
  - The flares are a 10 x 10 frame sample reduced to one pixel, which the EE downloads about once per VBlank.
- **IN-407:** small untextured sprites go to the CPU (the old core's IN-348). It helps a little; most night palettes
  are textured.
- **IN-408:** CLUT loads from drawn targets, and textures over them, are read on the GPU with no readback.
- **IN-409:** indexed textures keep their indices, and the palette is looked up at draw time.
- **IN-410:** the recording is submitted every 96 batches.
- **Lessons from the variants (same build, switches):**
  - The GPU CLUT alone was slower: every texture with a GPU palette was decoded again each time.
  - Mirroring each of the ~500 memory CLUT loads cost a barrier each.
  - The submissions cut the GPU waits from 7-9 to about 2 ms per VBlank.
- **Night race at 3x, headless, per VBlank (287 -> 288):**
  - whole emulator 20.8-26.4 -> 17.0-20.6 ms;
  - GS thread 15.3-21.6 -> 13.6-17.3 ms;
  - readback 6.9-8.7 -> 0.8-1.6 ms;
  - CLUT 4.2-7.1 -> 0.9-1.4 ms.
  - Frames 7200-9000 are byte-identical between the two builds.
- **Checks:** new fixtures `hw_core2_cpu_sprites` and `hw_core2_gpu_clut`. CTest 248/249 in both interlock modes.
  Candidate 288 frozen (gt4vulkan.exe sha256 f0903b82...).
- **Next in phase B:**
  - the GS thread's CPU work (batching about 7-9 ms, texture 1.5-2.7, targets 2, uploads 2);
  - target churn (5.5 created per VBlank);
  - the memory budget.
