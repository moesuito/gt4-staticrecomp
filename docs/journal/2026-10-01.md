## Decoder expansion (M6 continuation)

- Expanded the decoder from 18 to 39 operations, driven by the unsupported
  report of the ten regions: SLT, SLTU, DADDU; LH, SB, LD, SD; BEQL, BNEL,
  BLEZ, BGTZ; the eight REGIMM branches; JALR and SYSCALL.
- Two mistakes were caught by the tests before verification: opcode `0x21` is
  LH, not ADDI (ADDI was never observed), and `0xffffffff` became a valid SD
  encoding, so it was replaced as a negative fixture.
- Region support improved from 177 to 71 unsupported words; nine of ten regions
  are now fully supported. The entry window `0x00100008` remains wholly
  unsupported (COP1/MMI/MTSAH/SYNC/MTHI/MTLO), deliberately deferred.
- Fixtures: 58 hand-selected supported words and 19 unsupported encodings,
  several taken unchanged from real region words.
