  that other fragments of the batch had or had not written yet; at scale even the 1:1 pass reads a neighbouring
  sub-pixel, because the 12.4 texture coordinate is coarser than 1/12 pixel. Spec 8.8 asks for a copy of the sampled
  region first; with it two 12x runs are identical (31 of 31) and 3x frames equal 332. Cost at 12x: +1.3 ms GPU
  (+6%), mostly the full-screen pass. New CTest `hw_core2_self_texture`.
