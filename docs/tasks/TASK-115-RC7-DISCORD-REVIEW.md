## Findings

1. `discord_presence.rs` uses synchronous `WriteFile` without a timeout or cancellation (lines 76-88). `stop_session` synchronously joins its worker (278-286), called by `set_setting_of`, the exit watcher and launcher exit while the shared AppState lock is held (`commands.rs` 317-320, 548; `main.rs` 280). A stalled or non-reading pipe can thus freeze settings/launcher state and block cleanup indefinitely. Worker-thread placement alone does not guarantee bounded responsiveness. `stop_game` clears presence only later in the watcher, after log settling and card harvesting, rather than at stop request.
2. The implementation writes handshake and activity frames without reading or validating any incoming Discord frame, including handshake acknowledgement, error responses or connection events. A successful OS write is not evidence Discord accepted activity; no inbound messages are consumed. No documented evidence establishes that this fire-and-forget protocol is supported or robust; unread responses may fill buffers and prevent reliable disconnect/reconnect. Validate the selected protocol against current official documentation or a maintained permissively licensed implementation and implement/document its necessary full lifecycle; distinguish what can be verified without real Discord. Do not claim actual Discord presence until tested by owner.
3. Stopping is checked at the top of the loop, but not between a slow connect/handshake and the first activity write (305-319); a disable or exit during a pending connect can still transmit an activity after the stop request. `start_session` also checks and installs state across separate lock acquisitions, allowing concurrent stop/start to race. Require serialized state/generation fencing and deterministic in-flight cancellation tests.
4. The three fake tests verify payload strings and basic start/stop, but do not validate actual clear frame, reconnect, stalled transport, active stop, in-flight cancellation, expected timestamp on both pre- and post-enable activity, a real launcher settings path, or the wire protocol. The `disable_clears...` timestamp assertion can pass with only the pre-disable message. UI focus checks use `mock-data.js`, not the actual Rust settings payload. The delivery's strong lifecycle and bounded-I/O statements are not established.
5. `settings.rs` and `state.rs` have extensive line-ending-only churn (hundreds of lines), obstructing review. Restore original per-file line endings without reverting functional edits. The report does not include exact test logs/commands/counts or a verified real Discord result. Update it with precise evidence and outstanding uncertainty; update the top of `docs/STATUS.md` as required by the original plan.
