# GT4Recomp 1.0.0

**The first release: Gran Turismo 4 running natively on your PC.**

## New
- One-click installer and a launcher that sets the game up from your own disc image.
- **60 FPS** at full game speed in every scene we tested.
- Any controller works out of the box (Xbox, DualShock 4, DualSense, Switch Pro), with button remapping.
- Widescreen 16:9 and progressive 480p races, always on.
- Your saves and replays are easy to find and back up, and survive a crash.

## Good to know
- Racing wheels and ultrawide screens are not supported yet.
- Some license demos and menu backgrounds do not play.
