# GT4Recomp 1.3.0-rc7

**RetroAchievements for Gran Turismo 4, 7 controller response curves, and standardized trigger travel.**

## New
- RetroAchievements support for Gran Turismo 4: connect your account in Settings > General to track achievements and rich presence.
- 7 controller response curves in Settings > Controls (linear, quadratic, cubic, s-curve, smooth power, sublinear, logarithmic) to fine-tune steering and trigger feel.
- Standardized trigger travel across all gamepads (PlayStation, Xbox, generic) for consistent acceleration and braking.
- Gran Turismo 4 Spec II 1.10 side by side with Gran Turismo 4, with independent discs, saves, and replays.
- One Saves list for both games, each save tagged with its game, plus New save, Export and Rename.
- Portable build: unpack the zip anywhere and your data stays in that folder.

## Good to know
- Achievements are active for Gran Turismo 4 (v2.00) and isolated from Spec II playthroughs.
- This is a test build: real gameplay steering feel and online achievement unlocks remain for player verification.
- You need your own disc images. GT4Recomp ships no copyrighted game files.
