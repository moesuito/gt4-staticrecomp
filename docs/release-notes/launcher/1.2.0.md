# GT4Recomp 1.2.0

**Custom texture packs arrive.**

## New
- **HD texture packs**: drop a pack in the Textures folder (Settings > Graphics > Open textures folder) and play.
- The renderer is now called **GSdx**, and every GSdx picture option is available in Settings.
- The resolution follows your desktop automatically, and monitors show their real names.
- The game window starts black instead of white.
