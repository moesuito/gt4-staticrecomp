# GT4Recomp 1.3.0

**Gran Turismo 4 and Gran Turismo 4 Spec II side by side, with a launcher you can carry.**

## New
- Gran Turismo 4 Spec II 1.10 joins Gran Turismo 4: pick the game on the home screen; each game keeps its own disc, saves and replays.
- RetroAchievements support for Gran Turismo 4: connect your account in Settings > General to track achievements and rich presence.
- 7 controller response curves in Settings > Controls (linear, quadratic, cubic, s-curve, smooth power, sublinear, logarithmic) to fine-tune steering and trigger feel.
- Standardized trigger travel across all gamepads for consistent acceleration and braking.
- One Saves list for both games, each save tagged with its game, plus New save, Export and Rename.
- The launcher picks the graphics API and the best graphics card by itself (for example the NVIDIA card instead of integrated graphics on laptops); both can be changed in Settings > Graphics.
- The game no longer needs Vulkan: on older graphics cards it starts with Direct3D 11.
- English and Brazilian Portuguese, following the Windows language (General > Language to choose).
- Setup recognises the disc image you pick (Gran Turismo 4 or Spec II) and checks it before copying.
- Settings > General: choose the update channel (Stable or Beta) and install any version from the list.
- Portable build: unpack the zip anywhere and your data stays in that folder. The game can also be started without the launcher.
- Export logs (About) puts everything we need for a bug report in one zip, with your user name hidden.

## Performance
- The launcher now stops its background animations while you play. On weaker laptops it was using up to about 20% of the processor behind the game, which cost frames.
- Performance Mode no longer makes the game stop once per frame to wait for the graphics card: the game no longer pauses to collect the frame's graphics work (about 2 to 3.5 ms less per frame, measured on a night race), and the wait for the image itself dropped from about 1.2 ms to 0.
- In Performance Mode the night lights and the sun glare are now turned off. The glare is what made weaker PCs lose frames when driving straight into the sun. The rest of the picture is unchanged, and the glows also stop appearing on top of the rear-view mirror (a glitch the original game and PCSX2 both show).
- The Performance Mode description in Settings now says what it really does: the car icons next to the gap time can come out blank, the loading picture may be missing, and the night lights and the sun glare are off.

## Fixes
- On some Intel graphics cards Vulkan lost the video card when entering the showroom; the launcher now switches to Direct3D 11 and remembers it for the next time.
- The launcher no longer says the game crashed when you close it normally.
- The Render scale slider now goes up one step at a time: 100% is the automatic size for your screen, and each step above is one notch sharper (before, 125% could jump several steps at once).
- On integrated graphics the game starts at native resolution, and importing PCSX2 memory cards (8 to 64 MB) no longer closes the launcher.

## Good to know
- Achievements are active for Gran Turismo 4 (v2.00) and isolated from Spec II playthroughs.
- Gran Turismo 4 Spec II is a mod by TheAdmiester and contributors; full credits in About. GT4Recomp ships no Spec II files: you need your own Spec II 1.10 disc image.
- Your saves and settings are kept when you update. The game program is now called gt4.exe (Spec II: gt4-spec2.exe).
