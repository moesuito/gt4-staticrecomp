# GT4Recomp 1.1.0

**Ultrawide support: the game now fills any screen.**

## New
- **21:9, 32:9, 16:10 and more**: races fill your screen and show more to the sides, with the HUD kept undistorted.
- Choose the monitor and resolution the game opens on.
- The game's sharpness adjusts to your resolution automatically, with a Render scale slider for more speed.
- All picture settings in one place: Settings > Graphics.

## Fixes
- Updating from inside the launcher works now.

## Good to know
- Menus, movies and photo mode stay 16:9.
